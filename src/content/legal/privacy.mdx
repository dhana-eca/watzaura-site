## Who we are

Watzaura Infotech Pty Limited (ABN 32 702 553 857, ACN 702 553 857) is an Australian company based in Melbourne, Victoria. We operate this website, watzaura.com, and we make Plinth: a software platform used by sports academies, and the Plinth mobile app for iPhone and Android. This policy explains how we handle personal information under the *Privacy Act 1988* (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles, and, where it applies, other privacy law such as the GDPR and India's *Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023*.

You can contact us about privacy at hello@watzaura.com or on +61 452 551 758.

## This website

### What we collect

watzaura.com does not use cookies, does not run analytics scripts, and does not embed third-party trackers. Reading this site sends us nothing beyond the ordinary server logs (IP address, browser type, pages requested, time) that any web server records. Those logs are kept for a short period for security and are not used to identify or profile you.

If you request early access, we collect what you enter in that form: your email address, your organisation, your role, your country and the time you submitted it. We also record the IP address the request came from, to limit abuse of the form.

### Why we collect it

We use early-access details to reply to you about Plinth, to understand which academies are interested, and to send you the confirmation email you asked for. We do not sell this information, add you to a marketing list you did not ask for, or share it with anyone except the service providers below.

### Who handles it for us

- **Hosting.** The site and its waitlist database are hosted by Render Services, Inc. The waitlist database is held in Singapore.
- **Email.** Confirmation and notification emails are sent through Resend, Inc.

Each provider processes your information only to provide the service, under their own privacy terms.

### How long we keep it

Early-access requests are kept until you ask us to delete them or until we no longer need them to run early access, whichever comes first. Email hello@watzaura.com to have your request removed.

## Plinth

### Who is responsible

Plinth is used by sports academies to run their bookings, memberships, coaching and athletes' development. When an academy uses Plinth, the academy decides what personal information it collects about its families, athletes and staff, and why. In privacy terms the academy is responsible for that information, and Watzaura handles it on the academy's behalf and on its instructions. Each academy's records are kept separate from every other academy's.

The academy's own privacy policy explains what it collects and why. Elite Cricket Academy's is at [book.elitecricketacademy.com.au/privacy](https://book.elitecricketacademy.com.au/privacy).

### What Plinth holds

Depending on what the academy uses, Plinth can hold:

- names, email addresses, phone numbers and addresses of parents, guardians, adult athletes and staff;
- children's names and dates of birth, so they are placed in the right age group;
- emergency contacts, and medical notes a family chooses to give so coaches can keep a child safe, which we treat as sensitive information;
- consent records, including separate, optional consents for photography and for promotional use;
- bookings, memberships, payments, invoices and account credit;
- coaching notes, ratings, attendance and match figures;
- photographs and video, where the family has consented;
- device details used to send notifications, if you allow them.

We do not collect card numbers. When you pay, your card details are entered on the payment provider's own secure page and never reach our systems. We keep only the card brand, its expiry and the last four digits, so you can recognise a saved card.

### The Plinth mobile app

The app shows the information above to the people entitled to see it: parents see their own family, coaches see the squads they coach. In addition:

- **Signing in.** Sign-in is handled by Auth0 (Okta). Your password goes to Auth0, not to us or to the academy. If you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive only your basic profile from them: your name, your email address (Apple lets you hide it behind a relay address) and, from Google, a link to your profile picture.
- **Face ID and fingerprint unlock.** If you turn this on, your phone checks your face or fingerprint itself. The app is only told whether the check passed. Your biometric data never leaves your device and we never receive it.
- **Notifications.** If you allow notifications, the app registers a notification token for your device with us, so we can send booking confirmations, reminders and notices from your academy. You can turn notifications off in your phone's settings at any time.
- **Payments.** When you pay in the app, it opens the academy's payment provider's secure page. The app does not see your card details.
- **No advertising or tracking.** The app has no advertising, no third-party analytics and no tracking across other apps or websites, and it does not use your device's advertising identifier.

We do not sell Plinth information, and we do not use it to advertise to anyone.

### Information held overseas

Several of these companies process information outside Australia, including in the United States, Singapore and the European Union, and academies in India have their records handled for their Indian operations. Your information may therefore be handled overseas, where privacy law differs from Australian law. We take reasonable steps to make sure these providers protect it in line with the Australian Privacy Principles.

### How long Plinth keeps it

- Financial records (bookings, payments, invoices and credit) are kept for at least five years, because tax law requires it. They cannot be deleted on request; when an account is deleted they are kept without the person's name attached.
- Coaching records are kept while the athlete is with the academy, and for a reasonable period afterwards in case they return.
- Photos and video are kept until the family asks for them to be removed or deletes their account.
- Everything else is erased when the account is deleted.

### Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the Plinth app (**Account** tab → **Delete my account**), or without the app by following our [account deletion](/legal/account-deletion) page. That page lists exactly what is erased and what has to be kept.

## Children

Plinth holds records about children because academies coach children. Plinth accounts are held by adults: a parent or guardian holds the account and gives the consents for their child. Photography and promotional-use consents are asked separately and are optional, and can be changed at any time.

## Keeping it safe

Access to Plinth information is limited to the people who need it for their role, and what each role can see is enforced by the system, not by hiding buttons. Information is encrypted in transit between your device and Plinth. No system is perfect: if something happens that puts your information at serious risk, we and the academy will tell you, as the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme requires.

## Your rights

You can ask what personal information is held about you, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted. For Plinth records, the academy controls the record, so contact the academy first; if you cannot reach them, contact us at hello@watzaura.com and we will help. We normally respond within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with the response, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at [oaic.gov.au](https://www.oaic.gov.au).

## Changes

We will post changes to this policy on this page with a new effective date. If a change materially affects how we use information we already hold, we will tell the people it affects, including in the app.
