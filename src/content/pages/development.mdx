sections:
  why:
    eyebrow: Why it exists
    heading: A parent pays for progress. Show it to them.
    body: "Academies lose athletes when nobody can say what changed. Not because the coaching was poor — because the evidence lived in the coach's head, a WhatsApp thread and a clipboard. Plinth puts it on the record, in a form a coach can fill in from the lane in under a minute, and a parent can read on the way home."
  pathway:
    eyebrow: The pathway
    heading: From intake to progression.
    items:
      - meta: Intake
        title: Player profile
        body: Playing role, batting and bowling style, squad, school, club and level, years playing, focus areas and the athlete's own goals — captured once, kept current.
      - meta: Baseline
        title: Assessment
        body: The athlete and family rate themselves at intake. The coach then writes the baseline — a summary, three priorities, an assigned training block, goals at three months, twelve months and long term, and what success would look like. Medical notes are held as sensitive data.
      - meta: Weekly
        title: Coach ratings by stream
        body: Batting, bowling or keeping. A short set of scores, a highlight and the focus for next week, written by the assigned coach from the lane.
      - meta: Between sessions
        title: Training diary
        body: Academy attendance is on the record from the roster. Between sessions, the family logs what the athlete did at home — type, duration, a note — so the coach sees the whole week, not just the hour they had.
      - meta: Ongoing
        title: Coach notes
        body: Dated notes against the athlete, with a flag for anything that needs a director's eye. Coaches are assigned to athletes, so the right person sees the right players.
      - meta: Prescribed
        title: Training programs
        body: Programs written by the academy, targeted at squads or individual athletes, published with a date and marked complete per athlete.
      - meta: Library
        title: Knowledge bank
        body: Coaching plans, drills, references and diet plans as text or PDF, shared across centres and assigned to athletes. Internal by default; parent-visible when a coach chooses.
      - meta: Competition
        title: Match performance
        body: Fixtures, ladders and per-match performance pulled from competition platforms and the academy's own league, so training and match form sit side by side.
  visibility:
    eyebrow: Who sees what
    heading: The coach writes. The parent reads. The director watches.
    items:
      - title: Coaches
        body: Today's roster, their assigned athletes, each athlete's pathway in full, and a regular digest by email.
      - title: Parents and athletes
        body: The pathway on the family's app — ratings, highlights, focus areas, assigned programs and knowledge items a coach has shared — beside their bookings and payments.
      - title: Directors
        body: Coach-to-athlete allocations, flagged notes, and active and archived pathways across every centre.
