sections:
  bookings:
    eyebrow: Bookings
    heading: Resources, coaches and groups on one grid.
    body: "Lanes, bowling machines, courts and rooms are resources with their own hours. Coaches have availability and time off. A booking can take a lane, a coach, or both, for one athlete or a whole squad — and the grid shows what is free right now, per centre."
    items:
      - title: Resource bookings
        body: Lanes and machines as bookable units with opening hours per centre. A booking can span more than one lane, and the grid shows what is free right now.
      - title: Coach bookings
        body: One-to-one and small-group coaching against a coach's real availability. Time off blocks the diary; reschedule requests go to the parent to accept.
      - title: Group sessions
        body: Recurring squads with rosters and per-session attendance, plus a waitlist for full slots. A member's plan tells the roster what they are entitled to, and flags a mismatch rather than blocking the child.
      - title: Programs and camps
        body: Holiday programs sold as packs, with the child and the days captured at checkout so the roll is built before day one.
  money:
    eyebrow: Memberships and payments
    heading: Get paid the way academies actually get paid.
    body: "Most academy revenue is recurring. Plinth treats memberships, credits and casual bookings as first-class, and charges them on the rails your families already use."
    items:
      - title: Direct-debit memberships
        body: Memberships collected by direct debit, with pauses, plan changes and a clear next-charge date on the family's account.
      - title: Credit wallets and passes
        body: Session credits that settle when a group session is delivered, month-bound group wallets and casual passes — with a ledger behind every movement.
      - title: Card payments, two countries
        body: Stripe for Australian centres, Razorpay for India. Tax invoices carry the ABN or GSTIN the centre trades under.
      - title: Vouchers, promo codes, invoices
        body: Gift vouchers, promo codes with rules, and tax invoices generated for every payment.
  comms:
    eyebrow: Communications
    heading: Reach every family, and only the right ones.
    items:
      - title: Broadcasts
        body: Send to every family or to one centre, in-app, by email and by WhatsApp, with the recipient count recorded.
      - title: Reminders
        body: Session and payment reminders on a schedule, with the log to prove they went.
      - title: Inbox
        body: Enquiries and interactions logged against the account they came from, so the next person picking up the phone has the history.
  reports:
    eyebrow: Reporting
    heading: Numbers by centre, not a CSV to wrangle.
    body: "Sales by centre and by day, program rolls, membership and attendance views — built in, because the person running three centres should not need a spreadsheet to know which one had a good week."
