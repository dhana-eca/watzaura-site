  delivery:
    eyebrow: How it ships
    heading: Installs from the browser. Updates itself.
    body: "Plinth is delivered as an installable web application. It adds to the home screen on iOS and Android, opens full-screen like a native app, sends push notifications and keeps a shell available offline so it still opens on a bad connection. There is no app-store gate between a new feature and your families, and store listings are packaging of the same app rather than a separate product."
    items:
      - title: Push notifications
        body: Booking confirmations, reminders and broadcasts to every device a family has registered.
      - title: Offline shell
        body: The app opens without a connection instead of a browser error, and loads the latest data when one returns.
      - title: Home-screen shortcuts
        body: Long-press the icon to jump straight to booking, the schedule or the player list.
      - title: One codebase
        body: Parent, athlete and coach views are one application with roles, so nothing drifts between platforms.
