        title: Often the cleanest start.
        body: A spreadsheet of families, a roster and a bank statement is a perfectly good source. We import people first, then memberships, then the next four weeks of bookings, and run the two side by side for one billing cycle.

          - One sheet of families and players, any layout
          - Memberships set up against the real next-charge date
          - Coaches enter baseline assessments in the first fortnight
