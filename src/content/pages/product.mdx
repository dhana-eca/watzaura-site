sections:
  record:
    eyebrow: The record
    heading: One athlete, one record, every surface.
    body: "In Plinth an athlete is not a customer row with a booking history. The record holds the family account, the memberships and credits that pay for training, every session attended, every rating a coach has given, every note, every program assigned and completed, and the goals the athlete and coach agreed on. Operations reads it. Development writes to it. The parent sees it."
    items:
      - meta: Family
        title: Account, players, consents
        body: One account per family, any number of players, with consent captured per person — including media consent, so a photo cannot be taken without a basis on file.
      - meta: Money
        title: Memberships, credits, payments
        body: Direct-debit memberships with pauses, credit wallets, casual bookings, program enrolments, vouchers and promo codes. Every charge on the record it belongs to.
      - meta: Training
        title: Sessions, attendance, roster
        body: Group rosters and one-to-one bookings mark attendance on the athlete's record. A family training diary captures what happens between sessions.
      - meta: Development
        title: Assessment, ratings, plans
        body: A baseline assessment, weekly coach ratings per stream, notes, focus areas and assigned programs, building into a visible pathway.
  surfaces:
    eyebrow: Three surfaces
    heading: What you get.
    items:
      - title: Operations
        href: /product/operations
        meta: Front of house and back office
        body: Resource and coach bookings, group sessions, holiday programs, memberships and direct debits, payments in two currencies, communications and reporting across centres.
        points:
          - Lanes, courts, bays and rooms as bookable resources
          - Coach availability, time off and reschedule requests
          - Memberships via direct debit, credit wallets, casual passes
          - Card payments through Stripe (AU) and Razorpay (India)
          - Broadcasts, reminders and push notifications
          - Sales reporting and program rolls by centre
      - title: Development
        href: /product/development
        meta: The differentiator
        body: The athlete pathway. Intake and baseline assessment, weekly ratings by stream, coach notes, a training diary, training programs, a knowledge bank and match performance — all on the same record as the booking.
        points:
          - Player profile with role, stream, club, school and goals
          - Baseline assessment with priorities and 3-, 12-month goals
          - Weekly coach ratings with a highlight and next focus
          - Programs assigned from the academy's library and marked complete
          - Match performance imported from competition platforms
          - Coach digests and parent-visible progression
      - title: Mobile
        href: /product/mobile
        meta: Parents, athletes, coaches
        body: One installable app on iOS and Android. Parents book and pay and see the pathway; coaches see today's roster, mark attendance and write ratings from the lane.
        points:
          - Installs from the browser, no app-store gate
          - Push notifications for bookings, reminders and broadcasts
          - Offline shell so the schedule opens on a bad connection
          - Coach view built for a phone held in one hand
  foundations:
    eyebrow: Foundations
    heading: Built for more than one centre from day one.
    items:
      - title: Multi-centre, multi-currency
        body: Each centre carries its own timezone, currency and tax rules. Plinth runs Australian centres in AUD with GST tax invoices and an Indian centre in INR under GST/GSTIN, on the same codebase.
      - title: Roles that match an academy
        body: Directors, centre admins, business development, junior admin and coaches each see what their job needs and nothing they should not.
      - title: Consent and privacy by design
        body: Consent recorded per person, media tagged with the consent that authorised it, sensitive medical notes kept separate, and a public account-deletion route as app stores require.
      - title: Data you can take with you
        body: Exports are logged and available. Your record is your record.
