  cricket:
    eyebrow: Cricket academies
    heading: Where Plinth was built.
    body: "Plinth runs three cricket centres every day, so the cricket case is not a persona — it is the product. Lanes and bowling machines as resources. Batting, bowling and keeping as streams. Squads by age and level. Weekly ratings with a highlight and a focus. Match performance from the competition platform beside the training record."
    items:
      - title: Lanes and machines
        body: Lane and bowling-machine bookings by the hour, multi-lane sessions, coach add-ons and opening hours per centre.
      - title: Streams and squads
        body: Ratings by batting, bowling or keeping. Squads from junior to senior high-performance, with programs targeted at each.
      - title: Competition data
        body: Fixtures, ladders and per-match performance imported from competition platforms and the academy's own league.
      - title: Two countries
        body: Australian centres in AUD with GST, an Indian centre in INR under GST — one system, one login for the director.
  facilities:
    eyebrow: Indoor sports facilities
    heading: Courts, lanes, bays and rooms, with coaches attached.
    body: "A facility that also coaches — futsal courts with a Saturday academy, a golf-simulator bay with a pro, a tennis centre with squads — usually runs its resources on one system and its coaching on paper. Plinth treats a court like a lane: a resource with hours, bookable with or without a coach, that writes to the athlete's record when a session is attended."
    items:
      - title: Any bookable resource
        body: Courts, lanes, bays, rooms and machines with their own opening hours and pricing.
      - title: Casual and member
        body: Casual bookings by card alongside direct-debit memberships, credit wallets and passes.
      - title: Programs and camps
        body: Holiday programs and term programs sold as packs, rolled before day one.
      - title: Development, when you are ready
        body: The pathway is there on the same record for the day the facility becomes an academy.

  notfit:
