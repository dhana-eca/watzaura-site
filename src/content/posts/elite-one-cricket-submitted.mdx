On 30 October 2026 we sent **Elite One Cricket** to Apple and Google for review. It is Elite Cricket Academy's own app, and the first academy app built on {P}. When both stores approve it, families at Hoppers Crossing, Pakenham and Medavakkam in Chennai can download it and sign in with the account they already book with.

This is the project we have been most excited about. It is the point where {P} stops being something an academy opens in a browser and becomes something a family carries around with them.

## What families get

- **Their week in one place.** What is coming up for every child, in their centre's own time zone, with a reminder before each session.
- **Booking on the phone.** Lanes, bowling machines and courts for as long as they need. They can cancel or move a booking themselves, or book again in two taps.
- **Memberships they can manage.** Join a squad, or pause or cancel a membership, without calling the centre.
- **Their child's progress.** Coach notes, weekly skill ratings, a training diary, videos and resources, on the same record as the bookings.
- **Payments and receipts.** Packs, credit and every receipt in one place. Card details go into the payment provider's secure page and never into the app.
- **One inbox.** Confirmations, reminders and news from the centre, with a second parent able to sign in to the same family.

## What coaches get

Today's squads and 1:1 sessions on one screen. Attendance that can be marked with no signal; the marks wait on the phone and send themselves later. Notes and weekly ratings for each player, and the academy's knowledge bank, which a coach can share with a family in a tap.

## Why it carries the academy's name

Families do not care what the software is called. They care that it is their academy's app. So the app in the stores is Elite One Cricket, with the academy's crown and ball as its icon. Watzaura builds and runs it. Elite Cricket Academy owns its families' records. The same approach is open to every academy that runs on {P}.

## What happens next

App review usually takes a few days. Elite One Cricket has been submitted for Australia and India. As soon as Apple and Google approve it, the academy will send families the store links, and we will post them here.
